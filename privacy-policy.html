Privacy Policy for Domain Age Checker - Whois

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Koci App ("we", "us") provides Domain Age Checker - Whois (the "App"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

Data collection

The App does not collect any personal data and does not use third-party services such as advertising, analytics or tracking services. Content you create in the App is not sent to us. Deleting the App removes the data it stores on your device.

Address lookups

To answer a check, the App sends the address you enter directly from your device to public services; we do not receive it. The domain name goes to its registry over RDAP (encrypted HTTPS) or, when a registry offers only WHOIS or RDAP gives no date, over WHOIS (not encrypted). The domain ending goes to whois.iana.org. The site's host name goes to the Internet Archive to find when it was first captured, unless you turn this off in Settings. A short link goes to its own link service, once, to see where it leads. These services see your IP address under their own privacy policies. Your check history stays on your device until you clear it or delete the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows when it last changed.
